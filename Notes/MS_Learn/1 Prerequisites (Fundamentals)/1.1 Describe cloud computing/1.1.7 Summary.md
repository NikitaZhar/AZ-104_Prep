In this module, you learned about general cloud concepts. You started with an understanding of what [[Glossary#Cloud computing|cloud computing]] is. You also learned about the [[Glossary#Shared responsibility model|shared responsibility model]] and how you and your [[Glossary#Cloud provider|cloud provider]] share the responsibility of keeping your information in the cloud secure. You covered the differences between the [[Glossary#Cloud model (deployment model)|cloud models]] (public, private, hybrid, and [[Glossary#Multicloud|multicloud]]). Then, you wrapped up with a unit on how the cloud shifts IT spend from a capital expense to an operational expense.

## Learning objectives

You should now be able to:

- Define [[Glossary#Cloud computing|cloud computing]].
- Describe the [[Glossary#Shared responsibility model|shared responsibility model]].
- Define [[Glossary#Cloud model (deployment model)|cloud models]], including public, private, and hybrid.
- Identify appropriate use cases for each [[Glossary#Cloud model (deployment model)|cloud model]].
- Describe the [[Glossary#Consumption-based model|consumption-based model]].
- Compare cloud pricing models.
